CivicFix
AI-Powered Civic Complaint Management & Rapid Redressal System
🎯 SDG 11: Sustainable Cities ⚖️ SDG 16: Strong Institutions
"Report. Resolve. Improve Your City."
CivicFix transforms urban grievance management from an opaque bureaucratic backlog into an autonomous, transparent, data-driven civic contract. Using natural language processing, geographic proximity modeling, and automated department dispatch, CivicFix cuts ticket resolution latency from weeks to days.

01. The Problem vs. The CivicFix Solution

❌ Bureaucratic Silos: Citizens don't know which municipal department to contact, resulting in misfiled tickets and dropped requests.
❌ Duplicate Ticket Clutter: One pothole on a major arterial road sparks dozens of identical complaints, overwhelming staff.
❌ Black Hole Redressal: Zero visibility after submission damages public trust and civic engagement.
✅ Zero-Bureaucracy Intake: Plain-text description automatically triaged into category, priority, and department in <1 second.
✅ Multi-Factor Duplicate Detection: Haversine geo-distance + keyword Jaccard overlap groups redundant reports into collective petitions.
✅ Immutable Audit Timeline: Every status update is recorded with officer attribution, timestamps, and work order notes.

03. Key Architectural Innovations

🤖 Hybrid AI Engine

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash integrated with a resilient local rule-based heuristic classifier. Never crashes even without an API key.

📍 Spatial OpenStreetMap GIS

Zero proprietary Google Maps billing or API key barriers. Powered by Leaflet.js with category-coded pins and density cluster rings.

📊 Live Problem Analytics

Interactive Recharts dashboard tracking department turnaround, volume growth, and dynamic "City Problem Insights".

04. UN SDG Alignment & Impact

UN SDG 11: Sustainable Cities & Communities

Target 11.2, 11.6 & 11.7: Identifies hazardous road craters, malfunctioning traffic signals, and unlit pedestrian corridors to safeguard public transit and community spaces.

UN SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Target 16.6 & 16.10: Every grievance status update is tied to an immutable history table with officer name, role, and dispatch notes, eliminating bureaucratic opacity.
